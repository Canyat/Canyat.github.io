# 01. Comma

## 1. Usage

### ① Replace "and" "or" （替代and和or）

：She has long **and** dark **and** shiny hair.

-> She has long, dark, shiny hair.

### ② Sentence + "," + "linking word" + Sentence （用逗号和关联词连接两个句子）

：We were tired, and we really didn't feel like going anywhere.

### ③ "bracket" -- "," + "extra information" + ","（充当括号，添加插入语）

：This book, first publish in 1956, is still useful for student today.

<font color="grey"># 若将插入语放置于句首或句尾，则去掉一个逗号.</font>

：Similar to most people his age, he isn't really thinking about his future.

：He told me that he wanted to quit and become a painter, which surprise me.

## 2. Common Mistake

1. 不要在主谓之间添加逗号
2. 不要在“that”前插入逗号
3. 不要在无关联词的情况下，往两句子中间加入逗号

# 02. Colon

## 1. Usage

### ① Introduce a example or explanation（解释说明）

: He had one problem: there was no way to get there in time.

: There's just one rule here: do everything I tell you.

: General idea: specific example / explanation.

### ② List（引出列表）

: We've invited a small number of people:: Bill, Emma, Rachel.

: Tolstoy wrote several books other than war and peace: Anna Karenina, The Cossacks, Resurrection.

### ③ Speech（引出对话）

: He said: "Don't talk to me about this again."

: They shouted: "Be careful -- don't get too close to the edge."

## 2. Common Mistake

- ~~Dear John:~~ **Dear John,**

--> At the beginning of a letter or email. Use a comma.

- ~~Monthly Report January:~~ **Monthly Report January**

--> After a title or heading, don't use a colon.

<font color="grey"># 这两条可谓是直击中式英语啊XD</font>

# 03. Semicolon

## 1. Usage

### ① Semicolons are used to connect two sentences which are about related ideas.（连接观点相近的两句）

: The population of shanghai is around 24 million; Beijing has around 22 million people.

: We knew he was guilty; there was no evidence, however.

**Linking word & Semicolons, Commas（关联词与分号、逗号的关系）**

1. **'And', 'or', 'but', 'yet', 'while'** 

***=> Comma***

2. **'However', 'therefore', 'nevertheless', 'meanwhile'**

***=> Semicolon / a full stop***

: The population of Shanghai is around 24 million; Beijing has around 22 million people.

**--> No connecting word => semicolon**

<font color="grey"># 无连接词，则使用分号</font>

: The population of Shanghai is around 24 million, while Beijing has around 22 million people.

**--> 'While' => a comma is necessary**

<font color="grey"># 有"While"，需用逗号</font>

: The population of Shanghai is around 24 million; however Beijing is slightly smaller, with around 22 million people.

**--> 'However' => Semicolon / full stop**

<font color="grey"># 'However'，可以分号，也可以句号</font>

### ② List includes phrases which contain commas（列表中含带逗号的短语）

: We travelled to four cities: Santa Fe, which is in New Mexico; Denver, Colorado; Las Vegas, Nevada; and finally Los Angeles in California.

: Only three people showed up: Tom, the head of accounting; Maria, who works in the sales department; and Ellie, who is doing an internship here.
