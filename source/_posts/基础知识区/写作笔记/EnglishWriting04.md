# 结构

## Subject + Verb

写作时要遵循句子1S1V原则，即一主语一谓语。

## Empty Subject ( "it" & "there")

: It won't take long to get there.

: It's worth going.

: There's a mosquito on your nose.

: There have been several developments since the last time we spoken.

### It

1. **it + distances / times**

: It's not far to the metro.

: It's six thirty. <font color="grey">#现在是六点半-表时间</font>

2. **it + weather**

: It's sunny.

: It'll be cold tomorrow.

3. **Discribing situation**

: It's safe to walk around at night here.

: It's really cosy in here.

: It's good that you could join us.

### There

1.  **Something exists** <font color="grey">#熟知的There be句型</font>

: There is some salad in the fridge.

: There are several reasons why I have to say 'no' to this idea.

## Subject + aux verb + main verb

: They have bought a nice house.

: My sister mandy will call tonight.

### Aux verb + Subject 表疑问 / 倒装强调

: At no time did I suspect that he was the thief.
